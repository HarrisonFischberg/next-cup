A new look for brewing, plus fixes.

- The brew timer is now a full-colour screen with a cup that fills as you pour, and each step change is hard to miss.
- Start brew shows your steps as one card you can edit.
- Unsaved brews sit first in the Journal, above past brews.
- Explore is now roasters and your flavors. Brew help moved to each bean's page.
- Thirteen more brew tracks.
- Deleting a recipe or discarding an unsaved brew shows Undo instead of asking first.
- Hold the dose and water buttons to change them quickly.
- The Lock Screen timer keeps counting when a brew runs over.
- "Rate later" keeps the bean, grinder and grind you chose.
- Water hardness accepts a decimal comma.
