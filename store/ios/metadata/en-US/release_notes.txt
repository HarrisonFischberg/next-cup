Roasters near you, and a simpler Journal.

- Explore now shows the three coffee roasters closest to you, with how far away each one is.
- The roaster map opens on your area once location is allowed, and "Search this area" looks wherever you move the map.
- Tap a roaster for its address, phone, website and directions.
- Roasters you save get their own page with your beans and brews from them.
- One search box on Explore finds roasters and coffees, and can hand your search to the map.
- The Journal no longer shows "Tomorrow, change one thing". Saving how a cup tasted just saves it.
