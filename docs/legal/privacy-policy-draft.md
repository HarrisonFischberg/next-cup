## What Next Cup is

A free iPhone brew journal. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no
third-party SDKs. The developer runs no server for the app and receives none
of your data.

## What stays on your phone

Your brews, notes, grades, recipes, beans, roasters, photos, settings and
backups are stored on your device. Backups are stored on your device and may be
included in your own iCloud or computer backup according to your iOS settings.
You can export everything (CSV or JSON) and delete everything from Settings.

## AI features (optional, off until you add a key)

If you add your own OpenAI or Anthropic key, the key is kept in the iOS
Keychain on this device only. When you tap an AI action, Next Cup sends the
brew, your notes, a slice of your history, and any photo you chose (with
location and camera metadata removed) directly from your phone to the provider
you picked. The provider handles it under its own terms and may charge your own
account. Nothing is sent without a key and a tap. Removing the key stops all
AI calls. The developer never sees these requests.

## Location

Roaster search can use your approximate location while the app is open, only
when you tap "Use my location". You can type a city instead. Location is not
stored; only roasters you choose to save are kept, without your position.

## Photos

Photos you pick are read through the system photo picker. Next Cup does not
get access to the rest of your library.

## What the developer collects

Nothing. No identifiers, no usage data, no crash reports, no contact details.

## Children

UNKNOWN: minimum age to be decided by the owner. Next Cup is not directed at

## Your choices

Delete all data in Settings > Your data (with Undo for a short time), remove
your AI key in Settings > AI, deny location in iOS Settings, and delete the app.
