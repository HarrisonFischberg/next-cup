# Next Cup privacy policy

_Last updated: October 2026 · applies to Next Cup 0.1 · published by Rare Creatures LLC_

**Status: draft adapted from the studio's other app policies and checked against the code on 2026-10-25. Not reviewed by a lawyer and not yet published. Do not treat it as legal advice.**

Next Cup is an iPhone coffee brew journal. It keeps everything **on your phone**. It has no server, no account, no analytics and no advertising, and Rare Creatures LLC receives none of your data. This document says exactly what it stores, what it can send and when, and how to erase everything.

## What Next Cup stores (on this device)

| Where | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Next Cup's database on your phone | Your brews (date, method, recipe, beans, dose, water, grind, temperature, time, optional TDS, grade, taste, notes, optional photo), recipes and steps, brew methods, beans and roasters (name, origin, process, roast level and date, flavor notes, bag weight, remaining amount, freeze and thaw dates, optional photo), water profiles, your own taxonomy aliases, saved suggestions, and queued or finished AI requests (the request text and the answer) |
| Drafts | A brew you are typing, saved so a relaunch does not lose it |
| Backups | Up to seven daily copies of your data, kept on the phone |
| Settings | Units, your remembered filters, which AI provider and model you chose for each feature, and that you read the AI notice |
| The iOS Keychain | Your OpenAI or Anthropic key, if you add one. It stays on this device, is not synced, and is not included in exports or backups made by Next Cup |
| The Lock Screen and Dynamic Island | While a timer runs, the recipe name, the step and the time are shown by iOS from this device. Nothing is sent anywhere |

Next Cup does not store your location, contacts, browsing history or any account details. Your phone's own iCloud or computer backup may include Next Cup's files, according to your iOS settings.

## When Next Cup uses the network

Only in these cases:

- **AI features (optional, off until you add a key).** When you tap an AI action, Next Cup sends the brew, your notes, a slice of your history, and any photo you chose (with location and camera information removed) directly from your phone to the provider you picked, using your own key. Nothing is sent until you tap, and never without a key. The provider handles the request under its own terms and may charge your own account. Rare Creatures LLC never sees these requests. You can remove the key at any time.
- **Roaster search (optional).** When you search for roasters or match a bag to a roaster, the search text and the map area (or the city you typed) go to Apple Maps. If you tap "Use my location", iOS asks first, and your position is used for that search only. Next Cup keeps only roasters you choose to save, with the roaster's details, not your position.

Any other request is not made by the app. Photos are picked with the system photo picker, so Next Cup cannot see the rest of your library.

## What Rare Creatures LLC collects

Nothing: no identifiers, no usage data, no crash reports, no contact details.

## Children

Next Cup is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, so it has no age gate.

## Your controls

- **Export** everything as CSV or JSON, **import** with a preview that never overwrites silently, and **restore** a backup.
- **Delete everything** in Settings > Your data (it removes your data, drafts, backups and AI keys, and offers Undo for a short time).
- **Remove your AI key** in Settings > AI to stop every AI request.
- Deny location in iOS Settings; delete the app to remove its data from this phone.

## App Store privacy label (proposed, to confirm against Apple's definitions before submission)

**Data Not Collected.** The app declares no tracking and no collected data in its privacy manifest. AI requests and map searches go from your device to services you choose to use, under their own policies.
