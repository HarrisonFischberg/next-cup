Good questions

Do I need an account?

No accounts, ads or analytics. Your journal stays on your iPhone and works offline.

What does the AI do?

It reads bag photos, looks at a cup that went wrong, and suggests one change. It runs only with your own OpenAI or Anthropic key, and only when you tap.

Can my paper notebook come along?

Photograph a page. Next Cup reads it into a draft, and you check every field before saving.

Is it free?

Yes. Free, no ads, as a TestFlight beta for iPhone.
