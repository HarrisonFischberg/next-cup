Questions

Does it need an account?

No accounts, ads or analytics. Your journal stays on your iPhone and logging works offline.

What does the AI see?

Only what you send with your own OpenAI or Anthropic key, after a plain notice and your tap. Suggestions are proposals; nothing changes until you accept.

Are creator recipes copied?

No. Their parameters are restated in original words, with credit and a link back.

Is it free?

Yes. Free, no ads, as a TestFlight beta for iPhone.
