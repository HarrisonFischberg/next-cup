Private by default

What Next Cup keeps, and what it never does.

01 · Storage

On your iPhone

Brews, notes, photos and recipes stay on the device and work with no connection.

02 · AI

Your key, your tap

AI runs only with your own OpenAI or Anthropic key from the Keychain, after a plain notice.

03 · Tracking

None

No accounts, ads, analytics or social feeds. Nothing to sign up for.
