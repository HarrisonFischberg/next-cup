Questions

Does it need an account?

No accounts, ads or analytics. Your journal stays on your iPhone and works without a connection.

How does the AI work, and who pays?

It runs only after you add your own OpenAI or Anthropic key. Every suggestion waits for your tap.

Can I bring my paper journal?

Photograph a page and Next Cup reads it into a draft for you to review before saving.

Is it free?

Yes, free with no ads, as a TestFlight beta for iPhone.
