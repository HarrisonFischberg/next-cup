Questions

01

Does it need an account?

No. There are no accounts, ads or analytics. Your journal lives on your iPhone and works without a connection.

02

What does the AI do, and who pays for it?

It reads bag photos, diagnoses a cup and suggests tomorrow’s change. It runs only after you add your own OpenAI or Anthropic key, and every suggestion waits for your tap.

03

Can I bring my paper journal?

Photograph a page and Next Cup reads it into a draft. You review every field before it saves.

04

Is it free?

Yes. Next Cup is free with no ads, available as a TestFlight beta for iPhone.
