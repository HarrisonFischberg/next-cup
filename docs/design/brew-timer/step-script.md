# What the brew timer says, step by step

## Cone dripper

### Balanced cone

- **0:00 · Pour to 45 g** (Pour now). Add 45 g. Wet every ground and give the dripper a gentle swirl. Then: **Hands off. Let it bloom**. You should be at 45 g.
- **0:45 · Pour to 150 g** (Pour now). Add 105 g. Pour in slow circles from the centre outward. Then: **Hands off. Let it drain**. You should be at 150 g.
- **1:25 · Pour to 250 g** (Pour now). Add 100 g. Pour to the final weight, keeping the bed level. Then: **Hands off. Let it drain**. You should be at 250 g.
- **2:05 · Hands off. Let it drain** (Do it now). Let the bed drain completely. Then: **Hands off. Let it drain**. You should be at 250 g.

- **2:15 · Pour to 240 g** (Pour now). Add 60 g. Pour 60 g. Then: **Hands off. Let it drain**. You should be at 240 g.

- **3:45 · Hands off. Let it drain** (Do it now). Remove the dripper as it finishes. Then: **Hands off. Let it drain**. You should be at 300 g.

- **2:05 · Hands off. Let it drain** (Do it now). Let it drain fully, about 3:30. Then: **Hands off. Let it drain**. You should be at 500 g.
