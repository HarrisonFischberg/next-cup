# Competitor profiles — Next Cup evolve-001

Researched 2026-11-06 from public App Store listings (descriptions, IAP lists, "What's New" notes, visible reviews). Web content is treated as untrusted data: these are claims made in listings, and none was checked by installing the apps. Nothing below should be copied (code, assets, copy). Only *behaviors* are noted.

Already covered elsewhere and not repeated here: Hanko (hankocoffee.app), Beanconqueror, Filtru, beanbench.coffee.

## 1. Timer.Coffee
- URL: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/timer-coffee/id6449322787
- Platforms: iPhone, iPad, Mac (M1+), Vision Pro. No Watch app is listed.
- Price: Free. Optional tips are $1.99, $4.99 and $9.99, and the listing says tips "never affect feature access". 5.0★ (136 ratings).
- Does well:
  - Has 27 methods with step-by-step guides and a coffee/water calculator. It saves a **custom grind setting per recipe**.
  - **Live Activities on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island**, plus background timing and **audio chimes per step**.
  - A brew diary covers notes, rating, taste and extraction, with a **monthly progress review**.
  - Bean inventory uses AI label recognition. Since 3.8.5, it **asks the user to confirm an unclear roast date** after a bag scan. Since 3.8.4, the scan photo can be saved to Photos.
  - 3.9.0 added an **immersive brewing screen**, **undo for deletions** and rebuilt **freshness reminders**.
  - 3.8.2 added **post-brew ratings** (a prompt right after the brew), **diary export as Markdown** and an in-app Help Center.
  - Has "Pulse", a live feed of what the community is brewing, and community recipe sharing.
- Worse:
  - Account sign-in and email collection (the privacy label covers contact info and user ID). This conflicts with a private, no-account stance.
  - Community and roaster-page features add surface area. An "AI recipe check" was **removed** in 3.10.1, which suggests AI checks of recipes did not pay off.
  - No widgets, Watch app or Shortcuts are mentioned.

## 2. Coffee Book: Brew Timer (Alexander Logan)
- URL: https://apps.apple.com/app/id1512681263
- Platforms: iPhone, iPad, Vision Pro and Mac (iOS 26+). Apple Watch is supported through the Smart Stack.
- Price: Free with IAP. Plus costs $1.99, or $4.99, $9.99 or $25.99 a year, or $14.99 lifetime. A "Coffee Book" unlock costs $29.99, and there are tips. 4.7★ (83 ratings). The listing says no data is collected.
- Does well (the most feature-complete indie app in this set):
  - **Lock Screen widgets that start a brew timer in one tap**, a widget that opens a favorite coffee, and a Latte Art widget.
  - **Siri Shortcuts / App Intents**. Siri can answer "is this coffee stale?" and "how much is left?".
  - **Live Activity steps can be advanced without opening the app** (3.1). **Apple Watch brewing appears in the Smart Stack** (3.2).
  - **Freshness labels** for resting, peak and past its best (4.1.1). This is a glanceable bean state.
  - **Custom brew guides shared by link** (3.2), plus an "espresso compass" dial-in aid.
  - In 4.3.2, a **cups selector** scales a guide's amounts. 4.3 added a new first-run onboarding and lets the user manage which guides appear on the home screen.
  - Bluetooth scales (Acaia, Felicita, Bookoo and others), an AI bag scanner and an AI barista. Caffeine is written to **Apple Health**.
  - The Coffee tab lets the user "search, copy, delete, and brew from one screen".
- Worse:
  - The paywall is confusing: three unlabeled annual tiers and a separate $29.99 unlock.
  - Reviewers ask to **hide advanced fields** they do not use, such as grinder RPM.
  - A reviewer says **dial-in shots are not subtracted from the bag's remaining amount**, so inventory drifts.
  - Requires iOS 26.

## 3. Single Origin – Coffee Timer
- URL: https://apps.apple.com/bb/app/single-origin-coffee-timer/id1480168613
- Platforms: iPhone, iPad, Mac, Vision Pro and **Apple Watch** (watchOS 10+).
- Price: $0.99 paid up front, plus a one-time "Single Origin +" unlock (price not shown). It unlocks unlimited custom recipes, 500+ community recipes, history search, accent colors and icons. No rating shown.
- Does well:
  - Guided multi-step recipes include non-pour **action steps** (swirl, press).
  - **Imports recipes from Fellow brew.link codes and xBloom share links**, and shares custom recipes by a short code. This is interop with hardware ecosystems.
  - **A notification after brewing prompts the user to log tasting notes**, which helps close the rating loop.
  - Live Activities aimed at long immersion brews (2026.59) and a Watch app (2026.58). Step chimes were improved in 2026.68.
- Worse:
  - Paid up front with a further IAP, and there is no visible rating base.
  - The log is basic: no bean inventory, freshness or stats are mentioned.
  - A share-link fix shipped in 2026.60, which suggests the share links were fragile.

## 4. Drippe – Pour-Over Coffee Timer
- URL: https://apps.apple.com/app/id1528844321
- Platforms: iPhone, iPad, **Apple Watch** (watchOS 11.6+) and Vision Pro.
- Price: Free with ads. Premium costs $3.99 a month or $29.99 a year. "Unlimited" costs $9.99 and Remove Ads costs $1.99. No rating shown.
- Does well:
  - The pour timer covers bloom through drawdown with **target water amounts per pour**.
  - **Watch controls to start, advance and stop** the timer.
  - **9:16 brew share cards** (recipe, time, dose, water, ratio and rating), saved to Photos or shared to Instagram Stories.
  - AI recipe suggestions from a flavor description plus acidity, sweetness, body and bitterness adjustments. Since 2.6.7, these **factor in highly rated brews from the log**.
  - iCloud sync.
- Worse:
  - Ads, a subscription and several overlapping IAPs.
  - The AI features are paywalled and region-limited.
  - Recent release notes are generic ("comfort-of-use"), and there is no inventory or freshness tracking.

## 5. Brewly: Coffee Timer & Journal (Jerome Sommerfeldt)
- URL: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6738063344
- Platforms: iPhone and iPad.
- Price: **Free, "No ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases"**, and no account. 5.0★ (1 rating). This is the closest match to Next Cup's positioning.
- Does well:
  - **Sound and haptic cues** for the pour, wait, bloom and drawdown stages.
  - **Share recipes as text or a scannable QR code.**
  - A **collection of grinders**. The grinder and exact setting are saved for each brew, and the grinder can be chosen during recipe creation (1.5.2).
  - **Restart a favorite brew from history**, and **update a rating later from history** (1.3.0).
  - History can be filtered and sorted by rating or date, with bulk delete (1.5.1).
  - **Scale a recipe by coffee amount** so the water and the pours adjust (1.5.2).
  - **Backup & Restore to a file**, which can be opened from Files, AirDrop or Mail (1.5.2).
  - A **redesigned post-brew finish screen** (1.5.2).
- Worse:
  - Small user base, and no widgets, Watch, Live Activity, bean inventory, freshness or stats.
  - Reliability fixes in recent releases: backups failed, and restarting from history crashed.

## 6. Bloom – Coffee Timer & Journal (Parth Jadhav)
- URL: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6759914524
- Platforms: iPhone only (iOS 17+). Photo scan needs iOS 26.
- Price: Free tier capped at 3 coffees and 5 recipes. Pro costs $2.99 a month, $19.99 a year or $29.99 lifetime. 5.0★ (1 rating).
- Does well (the closest *feature* match to Next Cup):
  - **On-device bag-label scanning** of several bags and sides, with a review step before saving.
  - **"Flavor Windows" estimate freshness from roast level and age.** Version 1.6.0 adds **freshness and running-low reminders**.
  - **Finishing a brew writes the journal entry and subtracts the dose from the bag's remaining weight.**
  - **Grinders remember their last setting**, and a default grinder can be set.
  - A "Brew Coach" suggests changes for the next cup. This competes directly with Next Cup's single suggested change.
  - **Assessments can be added later**, which separates logging from tasting.
  - **Repeat a past brew** (1.6.0).
  - **Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets**, a Journal insights card, caffeine tracking and **price per 100 g** comparison.
  - Spotlight indexing of coffees and recipes, plus **spreadsheet export and backup**. Scaling works by dose, water or cups.
- Worse:
  - The free tier's hard caps (3 coffees) are restrictive.
  - No Watch, Live Activity or Shortcuts. It is very new.

## 7. Pour-Over Coffee Recipes Timer (Pareto / Yui Lau)
- URL: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1615750286
- Platforms: iPhone and iPad.
- Price: Free. Custom recipes need a subscription ($1.99 a month or $12.99 a year). 4.7★ (54 ratings).
- Does well:
  - An **animated pour visual with audio alerts**, and a large library of named and party-size recipes.
  - Free users can adjust grams and ratio on default recipes. Pause/resume was added in 7.1.0.
- Worse (the useful part is what users ask for in reviews):
  - One review says the **pour animation shows the total weight instead of this pour's amount**, and that the timer does not suit fast-draining filters.
  - The same review asks for **pre-step audio alerts**, a **look-ahead at upcoming steps**, the option to **hide unused brew methods**, ratings on custom recipes, recipe copy/paste and sorting, and an option to turn off the animation.
  - Subscription restore bug: restoring a paid subscription froze the app.
  - Custom recipes are paywalled.

## 8. Dialed In – Espresso Tracker (Andrew Mitstifer)
- URL: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6759168523
- Platforms: iPhone and iPad.
- Price: Free. Premium is a one-time $2.99 (iCloud backup, custom fields). The listing says no data is collected. 5.0★ (1 rating).
- Does well:
  - Each shot gets an **automatic extraction label from "very under" to "very over"**, based on ratio or time.
  - **Trend charts** of extraction time, grind setting and ratio across recent shots. Since 1.1.0 there are **per-grinder trend lines**, and grinders have **dial-matched steps with remembered settings**.
  - The timer can be in-app, or the user types in the machine's time. Since 1.0.1, **rating and notes are optional and can be added after saving**.
  - The only review praises the "graphical shot analysis" and how simple the app is.
- Worse: espresso only, very new, and no widgets or Watch.

## Also checked (brief)
- **Acaia Brewguide**, https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/brewguide/id1449417468. Free, iPhone only. A companion to the Pearl S scale. **Recipes are shared by QR code or link, and an opened link deep-links into the app.** A recipe can be sent to the scale. The only shown review complains that **a recipe does not show the coffee:water ratio**. The app is rarely updated (last update 2025-02).
- **Fellow (Aiden / Espresso Series 1)**, https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fellow/id6612024910. Free, iPhone only, **2.0★ (67 ratings)**. Brew profiles can be shared and brews scheduled. Users complain that **schedules do not run**, that **there is no "brew now" option**, that the copy-profile button does nothing, and about Wi-Fi pairing. They ask for **profile notes, local/iCloud storage, a searchable library, and water calculated from the coffee weight**. Lesson: unreliable hardware-dependent flows sink the rating.
- **Good Espresso**, https://apps.apple.com/us/app/good-espresso/id6443742140. Free, 4.1★ (7 ratings). It can **re-log a favorite recipe quickly** and has a community feed. A reviewer complains that **grinder settings saved in Settings must still be entered for every shot**, and wants letters allowed in grind settings (for example "4B"). No update since 2024.

## Concrete behaviors worth considering for Next Cup (each sized to about 40 min)

1. **Show this pour's amount, and the running target separately.** During a pour step, make the amount to pour now the main number and the cumulative target the secondary one. Reviews of Pour-Over Coffee Recipes Timer say showing only the total weight is confusing: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1615750286. Drippe advertises target amounts per pour: https://apps.apple.com/app/id1528844321
2. **Show the next step during the current one, and alert before it starts.** A small "Next: …" line, plus an optional haptic or tick about 3 seconds before a step change. Requested in a review at https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1615750286. Brewly advertises sound and haptic cues: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6738063344
3. **Prompt for the rating right after the brew, or later.** When the timer finishes, go straight to the rating, with a "rate later" option that posts a local notification after a few minutes so the cup has time to cool. Single Origin sends a reminder notification after brewing to log notes: https://apps.apple.com/bb/app/single-origin-coffee-timer/id1480168613. Timer.Coffee added post-brew ratings in 3.8.2: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/timer-coffee/id6449322787. Dialed In and Bloom allow an assessment after saving: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6759168523 and https://apps.apple.com/app/id6759914524
4. **Remember the last grind setting per grinder.** Prefill the grind from the last brew that used the same grinder, or the same bean and method. Bloom ("Grinders remember their last setting"): https://apps.apple.com/app/id6759914524. Dialed In 1.1.0: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6759168523. The Good Espresso complaint shows the cost of not doing it: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/good-espresso/id6443742140
5. **"Brew again" from history.** A one-tap action on a history row that starts the timer with the same recipe and bean, and with Next Cup's suggested change applied. Brewly (restart from history): https://apps.apple.com/app/id6738063344. Bloom 1.6.0 (repeat brew): https://apps.apple.com/app/id6759914524. Good Espresso (quick re-log): https://apps.apple.com/us/app/good-espresso/id6443742140
6. **A freshness state chip on each bean.** A glanceable label (Resting, Peak or Past best) computed from the roast date, shown on bean rows and in the bean picker. Coffee Book 4.1.1 freshness labels: https://apps.apple.com/app/id1512681263. Bloom "Flavor Windows": https://apps.apple.com/app/id6759914524
7. **Confirm the roast date after a scan when it is unclear.** After a barcode or photo scan, highlight a low-confidence roast date for the user to confirm instead of saving it silently. Timer.Coffee 3.8.5: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/timer-coffee/id6449322787. Bloom's review-before-save step: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6759914524
8. **Subtract every brew from the bag, including dial-in shots.** Finishing a brew decrements the bag's remaining grams, and the bean row shows "~N brews left". A Coffee Book reviewer complained when dial-in shots were not counted: https://apps.apple.com/app/id1512681263. Bloom subtracts automatically: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6759914524
9. **A Lock Screen or Home Screen widget that starts the last or favorite brew.** Coffee Book: https://apps.apple.com/app/id1512681263. Bloom widgets: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6759914524
10. **An App Intent / Shortcut, for example "Start my usual brew" or "How fresh is my coffee?".** Coffee Book (Siri can say whether a coffee is stale and how much is left): https://apps.apple.com/app/id1512681263
11. **Advance timer steps from the Live Activity**, for example a "Next step" button on manual-advance steps. Coffee Book 3.1: https://apps.apple.com/app/id1512681263. Single Origin uses Live Activities for long immersion brews: https://apps.apple.com/bb/app/single-origin-coffee-timer/id1480168613
12. **Share a recipe as a QR code or link, with no account.** Brewly (text and QR): https://apps.apple.com/app/id6738063344. Acaia (QR or link that deep-links into the app): https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/brewguide/id1449417468. Coffee Book (guides shared by link): https://apps.apple.com/app/id1512681263
13. **Always show the ratio next to the dose and water**, and let the user scale by dose so the water and pours follow. Acaia review (ratio missing): https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/brewguide/id1449417468. Brewly 1.5.2 scales by dose: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6738063344. Fellow users want water calculated from the coffee weight: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fellow/id6612024910
14. **Hide brew methods the user never uses.** A setting, or a long-press, to hide methods from the picker. Requested in a review at https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1615750286. Coffee Book 4.3 lets users manage which guides appear on the home screen: https://apps.apple.com/app/id1512681263
15. **Undo for deleting a brew or bean.** Use an undo toast instead of a confirmation dialog. Timer.Coffee 3.9.0: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/timer-coffee/id6449322787
16. **A trend sparkline per bean**, showing rating, or grind against rating, over the last N brews, so the user can see dialing in converge. Dialed In trend charts: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6759168523. Timer.Coffee monthly progress review: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/timer-coffee/id6449322787
17. **An under/over extraction hint from ratio and time**, as a gentle label next to the taste-based suggestion. Dialed In: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6759168523
18. **A brew share card image** (recipe, ratio, time and rating) made with ShareLink. Drippe 9:16 cards: https://apps.apple.com/app/id1528844321
19. **Export or backup to a file** (CSV or Markdown, opened through Files or AirDrop). This supports the private, no-account promise. Brewly 1.5.2: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6738063344. Timer.Coffee Markdown export: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/timer-coffee/id6449322787. Bloom spreadsheet export: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6759914524

Positioning note: Brewly ("No ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases", no account) and Bloom (scan, freshness, coach) are Next Cup's nearest rivals. Coffee Book sets the bar for system integration (widgets, Siri, Watch, Live Activity). Fellow's 2.0★ and Timer.Coffee's removed AI check are warnings to keep the AI optional and the core flows reliable.
